Biography

Dr. Connor Robertson is an entrepreneur, author, and host of The Prospecting Show, based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. His business and publishing work covers sales, prospecting, business systems, and entrepreneurship. He is the founder of Elixir Consulting Group and The Pittsburgh Wire.

His educational background includes Western University and New York Chiropractic College. His title reflects chiropractic education. This profile covers his business, publishing, and media work. See the official biography for the primary author profile.
